## 6. Functional Validation and Defect Management

My hands-on product knowledge complemented the project coordination activities, helping accelerate investigation and verification of important functional issues.

### Functional Validation

- Personally performed major test-case validations covering Wi-Fi functionality, ONT functionality, firmware features, IoT functionality, and R1 Mesh scenarios.
- Validated device behaviour against project requirements and customer-specific feature expectations.
- Documented validation findings and coordinated corrective actions with the relevant technical teams.
- Supported engineering and QA alignment to prepare firmware for customer evaluation.

### Defect Triage and Engineering Coordination

| Activity | Approach |
|---|---|
| Issue assessment | Evaluated findings according to severity and delivery impact |
| High-severity issues | Coordinated directly with development teams to obtain engineering fixes and personally validated the fixes |
| QA tracking | Shared validation results with QA for formal defect tracking and release-process follow-up |
| Minor issues | Coordinated with QA to raise formal bugs and track resolution with development |
| Retesting | Followed up on corrective actions and verification results |
| Release readiness | Coordinated issue status, validation evidence, and outstanding dependencies |

This workflow connected hands-on validation with engineering resolution and the formal QA defect-management process.

## 7. RAID and Change Management

Risk, assumption, issue, and dependency management remained central to delivery governance.

| Area | TPM Action |
|---|---|
| Technical blockers | Identified the responsible team, arranged focused discussions, and tracked corrective actions |
| Dependencies | Coordinated engineering, chipset, AI-agent, and QA activities against milestones |
| Schedule risks | Investigated root causes with the relevant teams and assessed recovery options |
| Resource constraints | Evaluated workload and project criticality when discussing additional engineering support |
| Scope changes | Reviewed priority, implementation effort, and validation impact with development and QA |
| Field issues | Coordinated technical investigation, corrective actions, and retesting |
| Release risks | Tracked defects, test evidence, and firmware readiness before customer handover |

New requirements were assessed with the relevant teams to determine whether they could be accommodated in the current release or scheduled for a subsequent patch or release. This supported clear prioritization and realistic customer commitments.

## 8. Release Readiness and Customer Handover

I coordinated release activities across engineering, QA, the AI-agent partner, customer stakeholders, and factory operations.

### Release Workflow

**1. Engineering readiness**

Coordinated firmware builds and followed up on required functionality and sanity checks.

**2. Validation and defect follow-up**

Reviewed available QA findings and AI-agent integration evidence with the responsible teams, tracking issues through resolution and retesting.

**3. Release documentation**

Coordinated firmware packages, release notes, internal QA reports, and supporting AI-agent validation reports for customer evaluation.

**4. Customer testing**

Supported customer validation by coordinating feedback, field issues, and required firmware corrections.

**5. Factory readiness**

After customer go-ahead, coordinated with factory operations on approved firmware files, relevant bootloader inputs, customer-specific housing and sticker requirements, MAC-address allocation, and delivery planning.

**6. Final handover**

Coordinated the final firmware package and supporting documentation for customer acceptance and deployment activities.

## 10. Lessons Learned

- **Dependency management:** Early visibility of chipset, firmware, integration, and QA dependencies supports reliable planning.
- **Issue ownership:** Direct coordination with the responsible engineering team helps accelerate investigation and resolution.
- **Change control:** Assessing implementation effort and testing impact supports effective prioritization.
- **Customer engagement:** Regular reviews and direct technical communication keep delivery expectations aligned.
- **Release traceability:** Clear firmware versions, test reports, and release notes support controlled handover and repeat deployments.
