Wi-Fi 5 Broadband Device Deployment

1. Project Overview

- Role: Technical Project Manager
- Project Type: Wi-Fi 5 Broadband CPE Deployment
- Delivery Timeline: 2 months
- Deployment Scale: 1M+ devices
- Status: Initial project delivery completed

2. Project Objective

Deliver a Wi-Fi 5 broadband device against customer requirements by coordinating project planning, firmware customisation, engineering, quality assurance, validation, and deployment activities.

3. Project Management Responsibilities

- Managed project planning, milestones, timelines, and delivery tracking.
- Coordinated engineering, QA, vendors, and customer stakeholders.
- Tracked customer requirements and firmware customisation activities.
- Coordinated testing, validation, defect resolution, and retesting.
- Managed project risks, issues, dependencies, and escalations.
- Conducted customer status updates and project coordination meetings.
- Coordinated release readiness, deployment activities, and customer acceptance.
- Supported resource planning and cross-functional team coordination.

4. Delivery Approach

Applied structured project planning, milestone tracking, stakeholder communication, risk and issue management, and cross-functional coordination to keep delivery aligned with the agreed timeline.

5. Outcomes

- Completed the initial project delivery within 2 months.
- Supported deployment of 1M+ devices with approved firmware.
- Delivered within the planned timeline.
- The approved firmware continues to support repeat purchases and subsequent deployments.

6. Key Project Management Skills

Technical Project Management, Project Planning, SDLC Coordination, Firmware Release Management, Stakeholder Management, Vendor Coordination, Risk Management, Issue Management, Dependency Management, Resource Planning, QA Coordination, Customer Acceptance, and Delivery Management.
