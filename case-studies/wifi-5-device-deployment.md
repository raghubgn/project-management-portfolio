# Wi-Fi 5 Broadband Device Deployment

## Project Overview

- **Role:** Technical Project Manager
- **Project Type:** Wi-Fi 5 Broadband Device Deployment
- **Domain:** Broadband CPE, Wi-Fi, ONT, Firmware Validation, IoT
- **Initial Delivery Timeline:** 2 months
- **Deployment Scale:** 1M+ devices
- **Status:** Initial delivery completed; repeat purchases and subsequent deployments continue
- **Customer:** ISP customer (name withheld for confidentiality)

## Project Objective

Manage the end-to-end delivery of a Wi-Fi 5 broadband device project, from project kickoff and requirements coordination through firmware customisation, testing and validation, customer acceptance, and initial deployment.

## Project Management Responsibilities

- Managed project execution from kickoff through delivery and customer acceptance.
- Coordinated customer requirements, project milestones, timelines, and delivery priorities.
- Worked across engineering, QA, vendors, and customer stakeholders to maintain alignment on requirements and deliverables.
- Tracked project risks, technical dependencies, issues, action items, and delivery progress.
- Coordinated firmware customisation and validation activities against customer-specific requirements.
- Facilitated communication between technical teams and customer stakeholders to address project questions and delivery blockers.
- Supported release readiness and initial deployment activities, helping deliver the project within two months.

## Testing and Validation

- Personally performed major test-case validations covering Wi-Fi functionality, ONT functionality, firmware features, and IoT functionality.
- Validated device behaviour against project requirements and customer-specific feature expectations.
- Communicated validation findings and worked with the relevant technical teams to address issues according to severity.
- Combined hands-on product validation with project coordination to support delivery quality and customer acceptance.

## Defect Management and Engineering Coordination

- Assessed validation findings and coordinated the defect-handling approach based on issue severity.
- For high-severity issues, coordinated directly with the development team to obtain engineering fixes, personally validated the fixes, and communicated the results to QA for tracking through the official release process.
- For minor issues, informed QA so formal bugs could be raised with the development team, then followed up on resolution through the official defect-management process.
- Coordinated communication between development and QA to ensure findings, validation feedback, and required follow-up actions were understood.
- Supported the transition from engineering-level fixes and validation to formal defect tracking and release management.

## Delivery Approach

1. Aligned project scope, customer requirements, milestones, and delivery expectations.
2. Coordinated engineering, firmware customisation, vendor, and QA activities against the project timeline.
3. Performed major functional and firmware test-case validations, including Wi-Fi, ONT, and IoT functionality.
4. Coordinated high-severity issues directly with development and validated engineering fixes; followed the formal QA bug process for minor issues.
5. Supported customer acceptance, initial delivery, and release readiness within the planned two-month timeline.

## Outcomes

- Completed the initial project delivery within two months.
- Supported deployment of 1M+ devices using approved firmware.
- Combined end-to-end project coordination with hands-on validation of key device functionality.
- Established a delivery and defect-management workflow involving engineering, QA, vendors, and customer stakeholders.
- Repeat purchases and subsequent deployments using the approved firmware continue.
