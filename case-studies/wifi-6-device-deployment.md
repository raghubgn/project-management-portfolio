I coordinated defect handling across development and QA, using issue severity and delivery impact to guide follow-up activities.

| Activity | Approach |

| Issue assessment | Evaluated validation findings based on severity and project impact |
| High-severity issues | Coordinated directly with development to obtain fixes and personally validated the corrections |
| QA coordination | Shared validation results with QA for formal defect tracking and release-process follow-up |

This approach connected hands-on product validation with engineering resolution and formal QA defect management.

## 11. Lessons Learned

- **Early alignment:** Clear customer requirements and milestone planning help align engineering execution with delivery commitments.
- **Structured validation:** Functional and mesh testing provide important feedback for issue prioritization and release readiness.
- **Severity-based issue management:** Prioritizing defects by functional impact supports efficient engineering follow-up.
- **Cross-functional coordination:** Consistent communication between development, QA, vendors, and customers improves delivery alignment.
- **Release governance:** Coordinated defect tracking and validation evidence support customer acceptance and controlled delivery.
