# Wi-Fi 6 Broadband Device Deployment

## Project Overview

- **Role:** Technical Project Manager
- **Project Type:** Wi-Fi 6 Broadband Device Deployment
- **Technology Platform:** OpenWrt
- **Domain:** Broadband CPE, Wi-Fi 6, ONT, Firmware Validation, Mesh, IoT
- **Project Timeline:** December 2025 – March 2026
- **Delivery Duration:** Approximately four months
- **Deployment Scale:** Approximately 500,000 devices
- **Status:** Completed
- **Customer:** ISP customer (name withheld for confidentiality)

## Project Objective

Manage end-to-end delivery of a Wi-Fi 6 broadband device program, coordinating customer requirements, firmware customisation, engineering, QA, functional validation, issue resolution, and release readiness to support deployment within the planned timeline.

## Project Management Responsibilities

- Managed project execution and delivery coordination across the approximately four-month project timeline.
- Coordinated customer requirements, milestones, delivery priorities, and firmware customisation activities.
- Worked with engineering, QA, vendors, and customer stakeholders to maintain alignment on project deliverables.
- Tracked requirements, technical dependencies, risks, issues, action items, and delivery progress.
- Coordinated validation activities, issue prioritisation, and follow-up with the relevant technical teams.
- Supported release readiness, customer acceptance, and deployment activities.
- Helped deliver the project within the planned timeline, supporting deployment of approximately 500,000 devices.

## Testing and Validation

- Personally performed major test-case validations covering Wi-Fi functionality, ONT functionality, firmware features, and IoT functionality.
- Conducted mesh validation covering R1 Mesh and R2 Mesh scenarios.
- Validated device behaviour against project requirements and customer-specific feature expectations.
- Communicated validation findings and coordinated with development and QA teams to address issues according to severity.
- Combined hands-on product validation with project coordination to support delivery quality and customer acceptance.

## Defect Management and Engineering Coordination

- Assessed validation findings and coordinated the defect-handling approach based on issue severity.
- For high-severity issues, coordinated directly with the development team to obtain engineering fixes, personally validated the fixes, and communicated the results to QA for tracking through the official release process.
- For minor issues, informed QA so formal bugs could be raised with the development team, then followed up on resolution through the official defect-management process.
- Coordinated communication between development and QA to ensure validation findings, engineering feedback, and follow-up actions were understood.
- Supported the transition from engineering-level fixes and validation to formal defect tracking and release management.

## Delivery Approach

1. Aligned project scope, customer requirements, delivery milestones, and timeline expectations.
2. Coordinated firmware customisation and engineering, QA, vendor, and customer activities.
3. Performed major functional and firmware test-case validations covering Wi-Fi, ONT, IoT, and R1/R2 Mesh scenarios.
4. Coordinated high-severity issues directly with development and validated engineering fixes; followed the formal QA bug process for minor issues.
5. Supported release readiness and customer acceptance to help complete delivery within the planned four-month timeline.

## Outcomes

- Completed the project within the planned timeline of approximately four months.
- Supported deployment of approximately 500,000 Wi-Fi 6 devices.
- Combined end-to-end project coordination with hands-on validation of key device functionality and mesh scenarios.
- Coordinated engineering and QA follow-up for validation findings through the appropriate defect-management process.
- Delivered a Wi-Fi 6 device program using the OpenWrt platform.
