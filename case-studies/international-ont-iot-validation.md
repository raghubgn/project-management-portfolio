# International ONT Interoperability and IoT Validation

## Project Overview

- **Role:** Technical Project Manager
- **Project Type:** ONT Interoperability and IoT Validation
- **Technology Domain:** FTTH/GPON, ONT, OLT Interoperability, IoT
- **Project Status:** Lab validation completed; next-phase ACS test-case discussions ongoing
- **Customer:** International telecom operator (name withheld for confidentiality)

## Project Objective

Coordinate interoperability and IoT validation of broadband Optical Network Terminals (ONTs) in an international operator's lab environment, working with the customer’s engineering team to validate the proposed functionality.

## Project Management Responsibilities

- Coordinated discussions with international customer stakeholders to understand project scope, technical expectations, and validation objectives.
- Participated in an onsite lab engagement to align with customer engineers on interoperability and IoT validation.
- Coordinated validation activities involving the customer's OLT environment and ONT devices.
- Facilitated communication between customer stakeholders and internal technical teams to track questions, dependencies, and validation feedback.
- Supported discussions on the next phase, including ACS-related test cases.

## Execution Approach

1. Aligned with customer engineers on project scope and lab validation expectations.
2. Coordinated ONT interoperability and IoT validation activities in the customer lab.
3. Reviewed validation feedback with the relevant stakeholders.
4. Continued discussions on ACS-related test cases for the next phase.

## Outcomes

- Successfully completed IoT validation in the international customer's lab with its engineering team.
- Established alignment on follow-up ACS-related test-case discussions.
- Built cross-functional and international stakeholder coordination experience across customer and internal technical teams.

## Project Management Skills Demonstrated

- International Stakeholder Management
- Technical Project Coordination
- Scope and Requirements Alignment
- Cross-Functional Communication
- Dependency and Issue Tracking
- Validation Planning and Coordination
- Follow-Up and Next-Phase Planning
